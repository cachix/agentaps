BUILT FOR HOW YOU WORK

Local. Remote. Wherever you are.

01 / ORGANIZE

Every project, together

Keep sessions in one sidebar. Archive conversations, fork a reply, and return to the work that matters.

02 / CONNECT

Work over SSH

Run agents on a server using your existing SSH setup. Follow the conversation from your desktop.

03 / REVIEW

See what changed

Inspect local changes in unified or split diffs beside the conversation, without leaving your workspace.

04 / CONTINUE

Take it to your browser

Read conversations, send prompts, and answer permission requests through an encrypted Iroh connection.

REMOTE CONTROL

Step away.
Stay connected.

Open the phone icon in Agentaps and scan its QR code. Your agents keep running on your desktop. Your browser goes with you.
